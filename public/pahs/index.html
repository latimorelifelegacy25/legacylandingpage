Our Story

A Saved Life Became
A Mission

December 7, 2010

Jackson M. Latimore Sr. collapsed from sudden cardiac arrest at ESU's Koehler Fieldhouse — and was saved by an AED placed by the Gregory W. Moyer Defibrillator Fund, honoring a 15-year-old boy who died from the same cause.

That moment — watching a prepared community save a life — is the heartbeat behind everything we do at Latimore Life & Legacy LLC.

We don't sell fear. We help families in Schuylkill, Luzerne, and Northumberland counties prepare for life's uncertainties with clarity and confidence — because legacy isn't just what you leave behind. It's how you show up today.

Supporting PAHS football is just one way we put our mission into action — right here, in our community.
